approval flows, and delivery coordination.

- campaign launch and go-live coordination
- media mix / budget restructuring decisions
- creative approval and delivery handoffs (e.g. to WPP/Wavemaker)

## Excludes

- the creative method itself (topic `DCO`)
- specific campaigns (they belong to their project file)
